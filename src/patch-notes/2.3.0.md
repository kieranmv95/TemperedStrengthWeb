# What's New in Tempered Strength, Update 2.3.0

Release Date: 11/05/2026

This update brings together everything we’ve shipped across the **2.1.x**, **2.2.x**, and **2.3.x** lines — from a richer home and workouts experience to Rainhill Trials, smarter program tools, and a more dependable app behind the scenes. If you’ve been updating along the way, you’ll recognise some of these pieces; if you’re catching up after a few releases, this is the full picture in one place.

---

## Home & Messaging

The home screen is now the hub we always wanted it to be. Alongside the welcome strip and improved program launcher from earlier in this cycle, you’ll find a **home announcement banner** with optional actions. Messaging is driven from **Tempered Strength (Sanity)**, so we can highlight news, tips, or calls to action without shipping a new build. If something looks stale, head to **Settings** and clear the cached Sanity app config — the Home tab will pull fresh announcements on the next load. We’ve also reduced flicker on **Pro-related banners** so status feels steadier when the screen settles.

---

## Programs & Scheduling

**Move program days** — You can reschedule sessions within your program when the week doesn’t go to plan. That’s phase one of session shifting: move individual sessions within the current week, with broader bulk moves still on the roadmap.

**Calendar & session chrome** — The sliding calendar at the top of the app is smoother and easier to skim across days. The **start session** control has moved out of the sticky header so it’s less in the way when you’re scrolling.

**Day switching & session flow** — Switching days behaves more predictably, and if you end a session early you’ll get a **prompt to finish the workout** so nothing slips through the cracks by accident.

**Notes** — The notes area on program day screens is cleaner (including the “copy notes from another workout” control), and previous sessions now appear in the **correct chronological order**.

---

## Rainhill Trials & Workout Content

**Rainhill Trials** is fully built out: dedicated workouts, **multi-difficulty** options so you can match intensity to where you are, and an expanded set of sessions with **updated descriptions**. The old “coming soon” placeholder is gone — the program is listed properly end to end.

On workout detail screens, **movement blocks** are separated more clearly so you can scan a session without everything running together.

---

## Workouts Screen & Discovery

**Disciplines** — A scrollable disciplines bar sits at the top of Workouts. Tap CrossFit, Hyrox, Pilates, and more to narrow the page quickly.

**Filters** — “All” and specific filters (Partners, Equipment, etc.) behave as a single selector: picking a filter clears “All”, and “All” clears the rest — no more contradictory states.

**Pilates** — **18 new standalone Pilates workouts** across Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced, from about 20 minutes up to an hour. They live on the Workouts screen without needing to enrol in a program.

We’ve also added **tracking links** on new features so we can see what resonates and keep improving the right areas.

---

## Reliability & Polish

**Account & onboarding** — Updating preferences from **Account** no longer risks **resetting onboarding** for people who’ve already completed it.

**Sync & subscriptions** — iCloud sync **conflicts** are handled more gracefully, subscription refresh is a bit more dependable, and **notification** styling has had a pass.

**Analytics** — Logging **standalone workouts** sends more dependable events to **PostHog**, with cleaner typing on our side so we can trust the data.

**Copy & layout** — Wording and layout are clearer across **home**, **records**, **workouts**, **settings**, the **program launcher**, **awards**, and **sync-related** flows — small changes that add up to a calmer day-to-day experience.
